## Question

When does a CP-SAT scheduler change outcomes, verdicts or advice compared with the greedy planner,
and at what latency? Expected: hybrid ≈ greedy except in tight or infeasible cells; pure CP-SAT
differs in day order. Every cell is reported, including where CP-SAT is no better.

## Arms (D72)

`greedy` (current planner with its oracle), `hybrid` (greedy first, CP-SAT when greedy misses the
deadline), `cpsat` (CP-SAT schedules every plan). Pure CP-SAT runs only on this subset, named before
any run because of its latency: curricula 4-node, 8-node, 8-node-x10; budgets 10 and 15; scenarios
calm, missed_day, deadline_cut (`PURE_SUBSET` in the runner).

## Cells

Worlds S0–S4; curricula 4-node, 8-node, synth-20, synth-50, 8-node-x10, synth-20-x10, synth-50-x10
(8-node-x10 content: AI-checked, human review pending, D88); budgets 5, 10, 15, 30; the W7 scenarios
calm, missed_day, budget_drop, deadline_cut, missed_2days, budget_crash, deadline_halved,
forgetting_21 (definitions and horizons as `docs/preregistration/W7_T4.1_2026-11-08.md`); arm
`adapt`; 200 learners per cell, seeds 1000–1199, paired across planners. `16node` is added by Plan
6's addendum if built.

**F1 × F3 subset:** each planner with `bisection_config()` (bisection diagnostic and confirmation
checks), worlds S0 and S4, curricula 8-node and 8-node-x10, calm, budget 15, the diagnostic first as
in the F1 grid. Greedy cells identical to F1 grid cells (same world, curriculum, scenario, budget,
seeds, config) are reused from the F1 results instead of re-run.

## Metrics

- Verdict of the first plan (FEASIBLE / INFEASIBLE / UNKNOWN) per planner, agreement between
  planners, and the UNKNOWN rate.
- Projected finish day of the first plan; shortfall days (exact for CP-SAT, bound or greedy based
  for greedy), i.e. the accuracy of infeasibility advice.
- Closed loop: true goal mastery, all-required mastery, minutes used, idle days, retention of
  reviewed concepts, reviews in the first plan.
- Executed prerequisite violations (D46, primary safety, must be 0).
- Planning latency per call (mean ± sd, cold-start plan per curriculum and planner).
- Paired bootstrap CIs (`experiments/stats.py`) for hybrid − greedy and cpsat − greedy on goal
  mastery and minutes.

## Hypotheses (reported whatever the outcome)

- H1 (F3-TC9): 0 executed prerequisite violations for every planner in every cell.
- H2: hybrid's first-plan verdict equals greedy's wherever greedy's first plan is FEASIBLE; where
  greedy answers UNKNOWN (oracle node limit), hybrid answers FEASIBLE or INFEASIBLE.
- H3: on goal mastery, hybrid − greedy has a 95% CI that includes 0 in every cell where greedy's
  first plan is GREEDY_FEASIBLE.
- No direction is predicted for pure CP-SAT; any cell where CP-SAT loses on a primary metric gets a
  one-line explanation in the notes.
