## Question

Does giving each concept 10 exercises instead of 4 (same graph, same lessons) change the baseline
engine's diagnostic accuracy or closed-loop outcomes? This is a sanity baseline for later F1/F3/F4
results on `8node_x10`, not a claim that more items help.

## Metrics

- **Diagnostic (primary):** Brier score of the end-of-diagnostic p against binary truth
  1[θ ≥ 0.70] (W7 T3.1); items asked. Secondary: AUC, next-response Brier.
- **Episodes (primary):** goal true-mastery rate; executed prerequisite violations (D46).
  Secondary: mean goal θ, all-required mastery rate, minutes used, minutes to goal mastery, goal θ
  at equal minutes, idle days, Brier/AUC of P(correct) on executed attempts.
- **Contrast:** `8-node-x10` − `8-node`, paired over learners, 95% bootstrap CI
  (`experiments/stats.py::paired_summary`).

## Expectations (reported whatever the outcome)

- E1: executed prerequisite violations are 0 for both curricula in every cell.
- E2: diagnostic items asked stay within `n_max = 6` for both curricula.
- E3: no direction is predicted for Brier or goal mastery; any CI that excludes 0 gets a one-line
