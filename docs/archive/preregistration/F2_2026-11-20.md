## Question

Does running the engine on a fitted item model (class guess/slip from real answer data) instead of
the D6 heuristic change diagnostic accuracy, next-answer prediction and closed-loop outcomes? This
is Stage 1 of D61 (sweep diagnostic only). Stage 2, the 2×2 with the bisection diagnostic, is
pre-registered and run in Plan 3.

## Seeds and cells

- **Diagnostic:** 250 learners per cell, odd seeds 101–599 (as the W7 diagnostic matrix).
- **Episodes:** arm `adapt`, scenario `calm`, budget 15 min/day, 200 learners per cell, seeds
  1000–1199 (as the W7 grid).

## Metrics

- **Next-answer prediction (primary for F2):** after the diagnostic, every exercise is answered
  once more by an independent draw; Brier, log-loss, AUC and ECE (10 equal-width bins) of the
  engine's P(correct).
- **Diagnostic (primary):** Brier of the end-of-diagnostic p against 1[θ ≥ 0.70]; items asked.
- **Episodes (primary safety):** executed prerequisite violations (D46). Secondary: goal
  true-mastery rate, all-required mastery rate, minutes used, Brier/AUC/ECE of P(correct) on
  executed attempts.
- **Contrast:** each fitted profile − heuristic, paired over learners, 95% bootstrap CI
  (`experiments/stats.py::paired_summary`) for diagnostic Brier, next-answer Brier, items, goal
  mastery and minutes.

## Hypotheses (reported whatever the outcome)

- H1 (F2-TC6): 0 executed prerequisite violations for every adapt cell under every profile.
- H2: in S4-assist09, `fitted_assist09` has lower next-answer Brier than `heuristic` (paired CI
  below 0) on every curriculum.
- H3: in S0–S3, no direction is predicted; W7 found the sweep slightly worse with fitted numbers
  in S0. Any CI that excludes 0 gets a one-line explanation in the notes.
- H4: the fitted profiles ask at least as many diagnostic items as the heuristic on average (each
  answer carries less information, so the entropy stop comes later).
