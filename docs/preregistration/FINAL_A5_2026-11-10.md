## 2. B.2 — per-world calibration profiles

- **Data:** `static_loop` episodes (state-independent, covers every difficulty), worlds **S0, S1, S4**, curricula 4-node and 8-node, B = 15, scenario calm, horizon 14 days, nominal profile, fit seeds.
- **Model:** BKT with lessons: prior p0, T_lesson (transition after a lesson; newly fitted), T_exercise, guess/slip per D6 difficulty class (easy/medium/hard); no forgetting (S3 `forgets` is O.3, separate). One sequence per (learner, concept).
- **Fit:** Baum–Welch EM, best of 5 random restarts (pyBKT init ranges, numpy seed 42), stop at |ΔLL| ≤ 0.005 or 200 iterations.
- **Degenerate-fit check:** flags guess + slip ≥ 1, guess > 0.5, slip > 0.5, prior < 0.01 are recorded. A class with guess + slip ≥ 1 makes the profile invalid; an invalid profile is dropped from B.3 (logged).
- **Output:** `config/fitted_S0.json`, `config/fitted_S1.json`, `config/fitted_S4.json` (source, seeds, code hash) and `experiments/results/w8_fits.json`.

## 3. B.3 — selection rule (applied exactly as written)

**Candidates** (per world, "fitted" = that world's B.2 profile):

| ID | Profile | k_responses | θ | horizon-aware (C.3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| F2 | fitted | 3 | 0.85 | off |
| F3 | fitted | 2 | 0.95 | off |
| F4 | fitted | 3 | 0.95 | off |
| F5 | fitted | 2 | 0.85 | on |

Every candidate runs with consolidation (C.1) and the verification probe (C.2) **on**, the configuration adapt-final will use.

**Selection runs:** `adapt` closed loop, worlds S0, S1, S4 × curricula 4-node, 8-node, B = 15, calm, horizon 14, selection seeds (200 per cell); `static_loop` on the same seeds for the secondary metric.

**Primary metric:** in-loop Brier score of the engine's P(correct) on every executed exercise, pooled within a cell, then averaged over the 6 cells with equal weight. Lower is better.

**Rule:** the winner is the candidate with the lowest primary metric. If other candidates are within **0.002** of that minimum, the winner among them is the one with the highest **secondary** metric: mean over the 6 cells of the paired difference in goal mastery (adapt − static_loop). Remaining ties: table order. The winner is named in `experiments/results/w8_calibration_select.json` before any odd-seed run.

- **Worlds:** S0, S1, S4, S4-fitted (`s4_fitted_params(config/fitted_assist09.json)`, W7 T7.5).
- **Curricula:** 4-node, 8-node (Junyi only if O.5 data arrives; otherwise a deviation).

- **N:** 200 odd seeds per cell; headline cell **S1 | 4-node | B = 15 | calm | 14 days** at N = 1000.
- **Primary outcomes:** executed prerequisite violations (must be 0 for every adapt arm), goal mastery vs `static_loop` (paired difference, 95% bootstrap CI), all-required mastery.
- **Secondary:** false-mastery rate (pooled), minute utilisation, Kaplan–Meier median and restricted mean time-to-goal-mastery (τ = minutes available), in-loop Brier/AUC and reliability.
- **Statistics:** paired bootstrap 95% CIs (2000 resamples, seed 20260). No multiplicity correction; every cell is reported, wins and losses.

## 6. WP-J policy evaluation (J.6)

- **Training (J.5):** one `bandit_updating` policy (LinUCB + softmax, λ = 1, α = 0.3, τ = 0.1, zero initial weights) shared across learners, run sequentially over the training seeds in S1 and S4 × 4-node and 8-node (B = 15, calm, 14 days) on `adapt_final`; final weights saved as `config/policy_frozen.json`.
- **Reward:** learning progress: change in the concept's rolling success rate over its last w = 4 attempts after the chosen activity, clipped to [−1, 1]; 0 for a first attempt; a lesson's reward is assigned at the next exercise on that concept (0 if none).
- **Evaluation:** S1, S4 × 4-node, 8-node, B = 15, calm, 14 days, odd seeds N = 200 paired; variants deterministic, bandit_frozen, bandit_updating (starting from the frozen weights, per learner), random. Primary: goal and all-required mastery vs deterministic. Secondary: executed violations (must be 0), KM time-to-mastery, mean learning progress.
- **OPE:** value = mean per-decision reward. Estimate the frozen policy's value from the random and updating logs with IPS, SNIPS and DR (ridge reward model on the logged features); compare with its directly simulated value; report the error.

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-10, before any fit or selection run; code hash unchanged `f09fb8e2…`)

**B.2 data source.** The recovery test of the lesson-aware EM (`tests/test_bkt_lessons.py`) showed that
p0 and T_lesson are not separately identifiable when the lesson always precedes every exercise of a
concept, which is the case in `static_loop` episodes: only p0 + (1 − p0)·T_lesson is identified.
The fit data is therefore `static_loop` **plus `random_eligible`** episodes (same worlds, curricula,
budget, scenario, horizon and fit seeds); `random_eligible` places lessons at random positions among a
concept's exercises, which identifies T_lesson. Everything else in §2–§7 is unchanged. No fit,
selection or evaluation result had been produced when this amendment was written.
