## Data

- 4-node curriculum, simulator world **S0**, profile `nominal`.
- Tuning cohort: even seeds 100–198 (50 learners). Held-out cohort: odd seeds 101–199 (50 learners) (same split as W6).
- `population_p0` for `prior_mode = "population"` = base rate of 1[θ ≥ 0.70] on the **tuning** cohort (S0 initial θ), rounded to 2 decimals.

## Grid (648 configurations)

| Setting | Values |
|---|---|
| `min_items_per_concept` | 1, 2 |
| `pruning_mode` | hard, soft, none |

| `concept_cap` | 2, 3, 4 |
| `n_max` | 4, 6, 8 |

All other settings at `DEFAULT_CONFIG`.

## Metric

Per-learner Brier score of every concept's p against binary truth 1[θ ≥ 0.70] at diagnostic time, averaged over learners (T3.1, `experiments/diag_eval.binary_score`).

## Selection rule

1. Eligible: configurations whose mean items used on the tuning cohort is ≤ 8.
2. Winner: lowest mean tuning-cohort Brier (rounded to 4 decimals).
3. Ties: fewer mean items; then the smallest tuple (`min_items_per_concept`, `pruning_mode`, `selection_scope`, `prior_mode`, `h_stop`, `concept_cap`, `n_max`) in the order the values are listed above.
4. Report the winner, the shipped default and prior-only on the **held-out** cohort: Brier, log-loss, AUC, next-response Brier, items, and paired bootstrap 95% CIs of per-learner Brier differences (winner − shipped, winner − prior-only).
5. Adoption: the winner's settings become `DEFAULT_CONFIG` if its held-out Brier is lower than the shipped default's held-out Brier; otherwise the shipped default stays and the result is reported as a negative result.

Secondary (not used for selection): winner vs shipped in worlds S2 and S4 (T3.7).
