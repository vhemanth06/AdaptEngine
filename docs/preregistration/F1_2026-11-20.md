## Questions

1. Does the bisection diagnostic estimate mastery better than the sweep, for which learners, and at
   what question cost?
2. End to end, does bisection with planner confirmation reach the goal as often as the sweep, in
   fewer total minutes, without prerequisite violations?
3. (F2 Stage 2) How much do the diagnostic and the fitted item model each contribute?

## Part 1: tuning (frozen before parts 2 and 3)

Closed loop, arm `bisect_confirm`, world S0, curricula 8-node-x10 and synth-20-x10, tuning seeds
2000–2099 (disjoint from every held-out seed below). Grid: `bisection_stop_confidence` ∈
{0.7, 0.8, 0.9} × `bisection_n_ceiling` ∈ {13, 20, 40}. Rule: highest goal-mastery rate over both
curricula; ties by fewer mean total minutes, then the defaults (0.8, 40), then smaller values. The
winner is used by every bisection arm in parts 2 and 3 and recorded in the results metadata.

## Part 2: diagnostic matrix (all concepts in scope)

- Worlds S0, S2, S4 (as the W7 diagnostic matrix; S1 and S3 have the same starting knowledge as S0,
  so their diagnostic is identical). Profile `nominal`. Held-out odd seeds 101–599 (250 learners).
- Arms: `sweep_p020` (D59 default), `sweep_p024` (`population_prior_config()` prior), `bisect`,
  `bisect_indep` (`bisection_prior = "independent_p0"`, comparison only), `prior_only`,
  `fixed@n` (round-robin at the `bisect` arm's item count per learner). The bisection arms run with
  `confirm_diag_confident` on so confirmations can be counted; the p values do not depend on it.
- Scripted learners (100 seeds each, odd 101–299; answers drawn from the heuristic item model):
  advanced (knows all), advanced_minus_goal, mid (first half of a topological order), novice;
  arms `sweep_p020` and `bisect`.
- Metrics: Brier, log-loss and AUC of p against truth 1[θ ≥ 0.70] (scripted: the scripted set),
  items asked, false-confirm rate (confirmed at the diagnostic but truly not mastered, over
  confirmed concepts), false-fail rate of true knowers (truly mastered with p < 0.5).

## Part 3: closed loop (the diagnostic runs first, on goal ∪ ancestors(goal), D54)

- Worlds S0–S4; scenario `calm`; budget 15 min/day; seeds 1000–1199 (200 learners), paired.
- Arms: `sweep`, `bisect_noconfirm`, `bisect_confirm`; F2 Stage-2 2×2 adds `sweep_fitted` and
  `bisect_confirm_fitted` (`model_profile = config/fitted_assist09.json`), and the four 2×2 arms
  also run in S4-assist09 (S4 driven by the fitted_assist09 numbers).
- Metrics: goal true-mastery rate and all-required mastery (primary); total minutes = diagnostic
  item minutes + executed plan minutes (primary); executed prerequisite violations (D46, primary
  safety); idle days; diagnostic items; confirmation false-pass rate (concepts confirmed by the end
  whose true θ < 0.70, over confirmed concepts); false-confirm rate at the diagnostic.
- Contrasts (paired bootstrap 95% CI): bisect_confirm − sweep, bisect_noconfirm − sweep,
  bisect_confirm − bisect_noconfirm (minutes saved by confirmation), sweep_fitted − sweep,
  bisect_confirm_fitted − sweep, bisect_confirm_fitted − bisect_confirm.

## Hypotheses (reported whatever the outcome)

- H1 (F1-TC10): 0 executed prerequisite violations in every closed-loop cell.
- H2: in the diagnostic matrix, `bisect` has lower Brier than `sweep_p020` for the scripted
  advanced and advanced_minus_goal learners on every curriculum.
- H3: `bisect_confirm` uses fewer total minutes than `sweep` (paired CI below 0) in S0 on 8-node
  and 8-node-x10, with a goal-mastery rate not lower by more than 0.05.
- H4: the confirmation false-pass rate is at most 0.10 in S0–S3 (ε = 0.02 is the calibrated target;
  the simulator's learners are not generated by the engine's item model).
- No direction is predicted for novices or for the fitted arms; every cell where bisection loses
  on a primary metric gets a one-line explanation in the notes.
