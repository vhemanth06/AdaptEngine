## Question

Does bisection with confirmation checks reach the goal as often as the sweep, in fewer total minutes,
safely? How much do the diagnostic and the item model each contribute?

## Arms

The diagnostic runs first on the goal and its ancestors (D54); then the greedy closed loop.

| Arm | Config |
|---|---|
| sweep | `DEFAULT_CONFIG` |
| bisect_noconfirm | `bisection_config(confirm_diag_confident=False)` |
| bisect_confirm | `bisection_config()` |
| sweep_fitted | `DEFAULT_CONFIG` + `model_profile="config/fitted_assist09.json"` |
| bisect_noconfirm_fitted | `bisection_config(confirm_diag_confident=False, model_profile=…assist09)` |
| bisect_confirm_fitted | `bisection_config(model_profile=…assist09)` |

The 2×2 is {sweep, bisect_confirm} × {heuristic, fitted_assist09}.

## Cells, seeds, N

Worlds S0–S4 and S4-assist09 × 4-node, 8-node, `8node_x10`, synth-20, synth-20-x10 × budget 15 × calm;
seeds 1000–1199 (N = 200), paired across arms. Each cell's horizon comes from `DEFAULT_CONFIG`, so all
arms share the deadline.

## Metrics

Goal true mastery and all-required mastery (primary); total minutes = diagnostic minutes + executed study
minutes (primary); executed violations (primary safety); questions asked; confirmation false-pass rate
= share of confirmation events (by the diagnostic's ε test or by a planner check) where the learner's
true θ was below 0.70 when the confirmation happened (θ before any later forgetting); secondary: the
share of concepts confirmed at the end whose θ at the deadline is below 0.70 (includes forgetting);
idle days; plan-level validity.

## Contrasts

Paired: bisect_confirm − sweep; bisect_noconfirm − sweep; bisect_confirm − bisect_noconfirm; the 2×2
main effect of the diagnostic (mean over item models of bisect_confirm − sweep), main effect of the item
model (mean over diagnostics of fitted − heuristic) and interaction ((bc_f − sweep_f) − (bc_h − sweep_h)),
on goal mastery, all-required mastery and total minutes.

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** 0 executed violations in every cell (all arms).
- **H2:** in S0 on 8-node and `8node_x10`, bisect_confirm uses fewer total minutes than sweep (CI entirely
  below 0), with goal mastery not lower by more than 0.05 (CI lower bound ≥ −0.05).
- **H3:** the confirmation false-pass rate (θ at the time of confirmation) is ≤ 0.10 for bisect_confirm and bisect_confirm_fitted in every
  S0–S3 cell that has confirmations.
- No direction is predicted for the 2×2.

## Explanation rule

Every cell where bisect_confirm loses to sweep on a primary metric (goal or all-required CI entirely
below 0, or total minutes CI entirely above 0) gets a one-line explanation in `NOTES.md`; the runner
lists them in `bisection_losses`.
