## Question

Does the engine ever teach out of order, and does adapting pay off compared with not adapting?

## Arms

No diagnostic: every learner starts at the engine's prior p0 = 0.20. Base config `DEFAULT_CONFIG`.

| Arm | EngineConfig overrides | Harness mode |
|---|---|---|
| adapt | none | closed loop: `Engine.next_activities` at the start of each day and after every wrong answer |
| no_review | none; review urgency 0 for every concept | closed loop |
| no_replan | none | first plan only (its best-effort days when not FEASIBLE), never replanned |
| static | no engine | goal and ancestors in topological order, lessons then exercises by id, next-fit packed |
| adapt_h7 (forgetting_21 only) | `half_life_days=7.0` | closed loop |
| no_review_h7 (forgetting_21 only) | `half_life_days=7.0`; review urgency 0 | closed loop |

## Cells, seeds, N

- Worlds S0–S4 × curricula 4-node, 8-node, `8node_x10`, synth-20 × budgets 5, 10, 15, 30 × scenarios
  calm, missed_day, budget_drop (not at 5), deadline_cut, missed_2days, budget_crash (only at 30),
  deadline_halved, forgetting_21. 560 cells.
- Learner profile nominal; fast_forgetter (half-life 7 days) in forgetting_21 cells.
- Horizon: new-content days of an uncapped `DEFAULT_CONFIG` plan (deadline 60) + 2, minimum 3;
  forgetting_21 uses deadline 21.
- Seeds 1000–1199 (N = 200), paired across arms. Headline cells (S1 and S0 × 4-node and `8node_x10`,
  budget 15, calm): seeds 1000–1999 (N = 1000).

## Metrics

- Primary safety: executed prerequisite violations (D46), per arm and cell.
- Primary outcomes: goal true-mastery rate (θ ≥ 0.70 at the effective deadline), all-required mastery
  rate, minutes used.
- Secondary: goal θ at equal minutes (both arms' goal θ at the smaller of their minute counts), minutes
  to goal mastery, idle days, retention of reviewed vs not-reviewed concepts (forgetting_21).
- M8: first-plan verdict (FEASIBLE / GREEDY_FEASIBLE / INFEASIBLE / UNKNOWN) and feasible share; plan
  validity of every plan and replan (prerequisite order with confirmation checks counted as the engine
  counts them, the daily budget in force minus minutes used today, the effective deadline) as
  plan-level violations and the number of plans checked; Brier, AUC, ECE (10 bins) and reliability bins
  of the engine's P(correct) on every executed exercise, also pooled per world for adapt.

## Contrasts

Paired over learners (same seeds), mean difference with a 95% percentile bootstrap CI (2,000 resamples,
seed 20260): adapt − static, adapt − no_replan, adapt − no_review on goal mastery, all-required
mastery, minutes and goal θ (plus goal θ at equal minutes for adapt − static); adapt_h7 − no_review_h7
in forgetting_21 cells.

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** 0 executed violations for adapt and no_review in every cell.
- **H2:** in the S1 headline cells (4-node and `8node_x10`), adapt's goal mastery is not lower than
  static's: the upper bound of the adapt − static CI is ≥ 0 in both.
- **H3:** adapt − no_replan goal mastery is above 0 (CI lower bound > 0) in every missed_day and
  missed_2days cell at budget 15 (all worlds and curricula).
- **H4:** adapt_h7 ≠ no_review_h7 in at least one forgetting_21 cell: the CI excludes 0 on goal mastery,
  goal θ or all-required mastery.
- **H5:** 0 plan-level violations for adapt and no_review in every cell.
- No direction is predicted for adapt − static elsewhere.

## Explanation rule

Every cell where static wins on goal mastery (adapt − static CI entirely below 0) gets a one-line
explanation in `experiments_v2/NOTES.md`; the runner lists those cells in
`static_wins_goal_mastery`.
