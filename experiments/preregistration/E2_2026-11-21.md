## Question

How well does each diagnostic estimate what the learner knows, and at what question cost?

## Arms

| Arm | Definition |
|---|---|
| prior_only | no questions; p = p0 |
| sweep | `DEFAULT_CONFIG` (no overrides), `Engine.diagnose(goal_concepts=None)` |
| bisection | `bisection_config()` at its defaults (uniform prior over knowledge states, stop at 0.80 on the top state, budget from the model with floor 6 and ceiling 40, at most 3 questions per concept, exact up to 5,000 states else 4,000 samples with seed 0), `goal_concepts=None` |
| fixed@n | round-robin over concepts at bisection's question count n for the same learner; p updated with the engine's model |
| irt_cat_global@n | external 2PL CAT (a = 3): max Fisher information item choice, EAP on a 201-point grid, P(mastered) = posterior mass of θ ≥ 0.70; one ability; n questions |
| irt_cat_per_concept@n | the same with one ability per concept |

## Cells, seeds, N

- Simulated: worlds S0, S2, S4 (S1 and S3 share S0's starting knowledge) × 4-node, 8-node, `8node_x10`,
  synth-20, synth-50; all concepts in scope (no goal); heuristic item model; odd seeds 101–599 (N = 250).
- Scripted learners (answers drawn from the heuristic item model, P = model(1 or 0, d)), arms sweep and
  bisection, on 4-node, 8-node, `8node_x10`: advanced (knows all), advanced_minus_goal (all but the goal
  and its descendants), mid (first half of a topological order), novice (none); odd seeds 101–299 (N = 100).

## Metrics

Brier, log-loss, AUC of p against true mastery 1[θ ≥ 0.70] at diagnostic time (scripted: against the
scripted set); questions asked; next-answer Brier (every exercise answered once more by an independent
`probe` draw); for bisection the false-confirm rate (confirmed but truly not mastered) and the
false-confident rate (flagged diag_confident but truly not mastered).

## Contrasts

Paired over learners: each arm − prior_only and bisection − sweep on Brier and questions asked (and
next-answer Brier for bisection − sweep).

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** bisection has lower Brier than the sweep for the advanced and advanced_minus_goal scripted
  learners on every curriculum: paired difference CI entirely below 0 in all 6 cells.
- **H2:** no direction predicted for questions asked, or for the simulated worlds.
- **H3:** every arm is reported against prior_only, including where an arm does not beat it.
