## Question

When does CP-SAT change outcomes, verdicts or advice compared with greedy, and what does it cost in
planning time?

## Arms

No diagnostic; closed loop. `planner_method` = `"greedy"`, `"hybrid"`, `"cpsat"`; CP-SAT frozen at
`cpsat_max_work_units=10`, `cpsat_seed=0`, `cpsat_use_greedy_hint=True`,
`cpsat_max_reviews_per_concept=3`, `cpsat_review_lateness_penalty=0.1` (the defaults). With
confirmation checks: each planner with `bisection_config(planner_method=…)`, diagnostic first.

## Cells, seeds, N

- greedy and hybrid: worlds S0–S4 × 4-node, 8-node, `8node_x10` × budgets 5/10/15/30 × the 8 scenarios
  (as E1), plus synth-20 at budgets 15 and 30, calm only; seeds 1000–1199 (N = 200). Synth-20 is

- shortfall probe (deterministic): cold-start plans for 4-node, 8-node, `8node_x10`, synth-20 at budgets

- Horizons from `DEFAULT_CONFIG`; profile as E1.

## Metrics

First plan's verdict (FEASIBLE / GREEDY_FEASIBLE / INFEASIBLE / UNKNOWN), agreement with greedy per
learner, UNKNOWN rate; projected finish day; shortfall advice; closed-loop goal mastery, all-required
mastery, minutes, idle days, reviews in the first plan, executed violations; M8 path feasibility (feasible
share of first plans) and plan validity over every plan and replan.

Latency (labelled timing; M8 runtime): cold-start plan time per planner × 4-node, 8-node, `8node_x10`,
synth-20, synth-50 at budget 15 (deadline = calm horizon), 30 repetitions after one warm-up, mean ± sd;
replan time per planner × curriculum × event (missed day, wrong answer, deadline change −1, budget change
to 10) from the day-2 state after day 1's work by seed 1000 in S0 (greedy closed loop), 30 repetitions;
one process, after the grid's pool has closed; CPU and load average recorded.

## Contrasts

Paired: hybrid − greedy and cpsat − greedy on goal mastery, all-required mastery and minutes.

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** 0 executed violations for every planner in every cell.
- **H2:** hybrid's first verdict equals greedy's wherever greedy is FEASIBLE; where greedy answers
  UNKNOWN, hybrid answers FEASIBLE or INFEASIBLE (compared per learner).
- **H3:** hybrid − greedy goal mastery has a CI that includes 0 in every cell where all of greedy's first
  plans are FEASIBLE.
- **H4:** 0 plan-level violations for every planner in every cell.
- No direction is predicted for pure CP-SAT.

## Explanation rule

Every cell where pure CP-SAT loses to greedy on a primary metric gets a one-line explanation in
`NOTES.md`; the runner lists them in `cpsat_losses`.
