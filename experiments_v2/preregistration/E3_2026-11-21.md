## Question

Are the engine's probabilities trustworthy, and do fitted guess/slip numbers make them more trustworthy?

## Part A — real students

- Data: ASSISTments 2009 skill-builder (`as.csv`, difficulty from `assist09_skill_builder.csv`) and the
  Cognitive Tutor sample (`ct.csv`) in `data/external/`, verified against `CHECKSUMS`; cleaning as
  `docs/data_manifest.md`. A missing or changed file is recorded as "not run: data missing".
- Models: heuristic D6 class (engine class guess/slip, unrated items medium; prior p0, learn T_exercise),
  fitted class profile (guess/slip per D6 difficulty class, shared prior and learn), fitted per-skill BKT.
- Fitting: fast EM, best of 5 random starts, seed 42, stop at |ΔLL| ≤ 0.005 or 100 iterations.
- Evaluation: 5-fold cross-validation by student (fold k seed 42 + k), difficulty recomputed per fold
  from training students; pooled out-of-fold RMSE, Brier, log-loss, AUC, ECE (10 bins), reliability
  bins, and per-fold metrics. `config/` is not rewritten.

## Part B — simulated learners

- Arms: `model_profile` = `"heuristic"`, `"config/fitted_assist09.json"`, `"config/fitted_ct.json"`;
  all other settings `DEFAULT_CONFIG` (sweep diagnostic, `goal_concepts=None`).
- Worlds S0–S4 and S4-assist09 × 4-node, 8-node, `8node_x10`, synth-20; odd seeds 101–599 (N = 250).
- Metrics: next-answer Brier, log-loss, AUC, ECE after the diagnostic; diagnostic Brier and AUC;
  questions asked.

## Contrasts

Paired over learners: fitted − heuristic (each fitted profile) on next-answer Brier, diagnostic Brier
and questions asked.

## Hypotheses

- **H1:** on both real datasets the fitted class profile has lower pooled held-out Brier and lower ECE
  than the heuristic.
- **H2:** in S4-assist09, fitted_assist09 has lower next-answer Brier than the heuristic on every
  curriculum (paired CI entirely below 0).
- **H3:** the fitted profiles ask at least as many questions as the heuristic on average: for each fitted
  profile, the average over all Part B cells of the paired questions difference is ≥ 0.
- No direction is predicted in S0–S4.
